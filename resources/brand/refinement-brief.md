# Logo refinement brief

Refine the original Snowztech snowflake and diagonal mark from the GitHub profile screenshot. Preserve the silhouette and upper-left-to-lower-right slash. Use consistent stroke widths, more generous negative-space channels, balanced geometry, and subtly rounded ends. Simplify cramped intersections and keep the mark recognizable at small sizes.

Output a single white mark on a solid black square with ample clear space. No lettering, browser UI, gradients, shadows, textures, three-dimensional effects, ornamental tessellation, or additional symbols.

Method: built-in ImageGen. This is a raster design concept rather than a finished vector identity.
